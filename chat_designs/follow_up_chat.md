8.⁠ ⁠Follow-up loop

24–48 hours after a confirmed event, Jio opens a conversation — one question at a time, not a compound voice note, matching the "warm, unhurried, short turns" persona:





Did you go?



How was it?



What was the best part?



Anything you didn't like?



Want more like that, or something different?

If the answer to (1) is no, Jio branches immediately: it asks only what got in the way — logistics, nerves, health, forgot — and stops there. No guilt, no repeated asking, no march through questions 2–5 about an event that didn't happen.

Answers update the profile and memory: the barrier and next_interest_signal are written into the rolling narrative summary, not mapped through hardcoded rules. The next suggestion cycle is generated by the same Gemini reasoning pass reading that updated memory — the adjustment is qualitative, not a lookup table. This keeps the suggestion engine's logic in one place (the reasoning prompt) instead of splitting it across rules and memory.

If the senior doesn't respond to the follow-up at all, Jio sends one gentle nudge within the 24–48h window and then drops it — no further reminders. Silence past that point counts as a non-response for that cycle, and repeated non-response is what the escalation rule below is for.

Hackathon note: there's no real multi-day wait inside a demo. The 24–48h delay is time-skipped by a manual trigger — a demo-only command fast-forwards the state to "day after the event" so the follow-up fires on cue during filming. The real scheduling logic (cron/delayed job) is not required for the hackathon build.

Escalation to caregiver

Jio notifies the caregiver when:





Three consecutive suggestion cycles pass with no response at all



The senior declines every suggestion for three consecutive weeks



Confirmed attendance drops to zero after a period of attending



The senior expresses distress, or mentions health deterioration

The escalation is a factual summary — what changed and when — not a transcript and not an interpretation. It's delivered into the same Telegram thread Jio already has with the caregiver from onboarding — no separate channel to build.

Hackathon note: distress/health-deterioration detection needs some classifier (LLM or keyword-based) to decide when it fires, which is a meaningful build item on its own and isn't needed to demonstrate the core loop. It stays as a stated trigger in this spec but is out of scope to actually implement today; the other three triggers (all pure counters on Suggestion/attendance state) are what the hackathon build should demo.
